The Four Experiences

One fort.
Four worlds.

Each hall is its own chapter — from the silver screen to a palace of mirrors, a royal court and a gallery of India's legends.

I

Ram Prakash Talkies

The Grand Film Experience

Two historical films, presented on the big screen inside the fort: Mystery of Nahargarh and Hadi Rani — The Ultimate Sacrifice.

  • Mystery of Nahargarh — “The fort remembers.”
  • Hadi Rani — “One queen. One vow. One immortal sacrifice.”
See What’s Showing
II

Sheesh Mahal

Exquisite Mirrors

A palace of mirror-work — ceilings, arches and floors glittering with inlay — where the royal court sits in state beneath the light.

Photography & video not allowed inside
III

The Royal Darbar

Feel Royal

Step into the private world of Rajputana royalty — gilded thrones, palace interiors and the figures who held court.

IV

Hall of Icons

Meet the Legends of the Nation

Freedom fighters, leaders, scientists, sporting heroes and screen legends — a walk through the people who shaped modern India, with family favourites along the way.
