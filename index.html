World's first Multi Experiential History Museum has become HISTORICA at a heritage site.

I

Ram Prakash Talkies

Mega Cinematic Experience (1878-79)

We have recreated the grand facade of Rajasthan's first-ever theatre, built by Maharaja Sawai Ram Singh II in 1878-79. Inside our Cinematic Theatre, history roars to life with cutting-edge visual effects, surround sound, and physical sensory immersion.

  • Mystery of Nahargarh: The untold story of Nahar Singh Bhomia, royal conspiracies, and why this fort was never inhabited.
  • Hadi Rani: The immortal Rajputana queen who gave the ultimate sacrifice to inspire her husband to ride fearless into war.
Immersive Audio-Visual Experience 1878 Facade Recreated Screenings Every 30 Min
See What's Showing
II

Sheesh Mahal

The Palace of Mirrors

The grandest and most exquisite jewel of HISTORICA. Step into a dazzling wonderland of over 2.5 million hand-cut concave mirrors, brought to life through centuries-old Thikri craft, pure gold, and gemstone embellishments.

Walk across the world's only Sheesh Mahal with designed glass flooring - an awe-inspiring spectacle where crystal chandeliers and royal court figures reflect endlessly into infinity.

2.5 Million Mirrors Centuries-Old Thikri Art Glass Flooring Strictly No Photography Inside
III

The Royal Darbar

Feel What It Was Like To Be Royalty

Cross the threshold into an authentic 18th-century Rajputana courtroom, adorned with pure gold and handcrafted miniature paintings celebrating the glorious lineage of the Rajputana Dynasty.

Encounter breathtakingly lifelike figures of the Royal Family of Jaipur in regalia, standing surrounded by ornate arches, gilded thrones, and royal guards standing vigil.

18th-Century Courtroom Pure Gold Artwork Jaipur Royal Dynasty
IV

Hall of Icons

Bharat Ratnas & World Heroes

A grand salute to the real heroes who shaped the destiny of modern India and the world. Set amidst meticulously researched historical backdrops and detailed period sets, this gallery houses over 42+ lifelike statues.

Walk alongside Mahatma Gandhi, freedom warriors, visionary scientists, world leaders, cricket legends, and cinema icons. Includes a lively dedicated zone where younger visitors can meet their favourite superheroes and cartoon characters.

The Visionary Behind HISTORICA

Anoop Srivastava

Founder & Director, Entertainment 7 Creative Ventures Pvt. Ltd.

A pioneer in experiential storytelling, Mr. Anoop Srivastava transformed a vision of cultural preservation into a living reality. From the inception of the Jaipur Wax Museum—the world's first of its kind within a heritage site—he has pushed the boundaries of how we interact with history.

By merging authentic Rajasthani craftsmanship, such as the breathtaking 2.5 million mirrors of the Sheesh Mahal, with cinematic technology, he has created an immersive universe where heritage is not just seen, but felt.

"Our goal is to ensure that the echoes of our royal past are not lost to time, but are brought to life with such vividness that future generations can feel the pulse of history."
