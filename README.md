# HISTORICA — Step Into History
### Nahargarh Fort · Jaipur, Rajasthan, India

> *रहस्य, त्याग और इतिहास — एक ही जगह*  
> **World's First Multi-Experiential History Museum at a Heritage Site**

HISTORICA is a one-of-a-kind experiential history museum located inside the 300-year-old **Nahargarh Fort** perched on the Aravalli ridge overlooking Jaipur. Formerly known as the Jaipur Wax Museum (inaugurated in 2016 by Bollywood star Govinda in collaboration with the Department of Archaeology & Museums, Government of Rajasthan), the museum has been reimagined 10X bigger by Mr. Anoop Srivastava and Entertainment 7 Creative Ventures (P) Ltd.

---

## 🏛️ The Four Magical Worlds

1. **Ram Prakash Talkies (1878–79)**  
   Recreating the grand facade of Rajasthan’s first-ever theatre, originally built by Maharaja Sawai Ram Singh II. Inside the 4D Cinematic Theatre, visitors experience physical motion seats, wind, surround audio, and two exclusive blockbuster historical films:
   - *Mystery of Nahargarh*: The untold story of Nahar Singh Bhomia, royal conspiracies, and why the fort was never inhabited.
   - *Hadi Rani — The Ultimate Sacrifice*: An immortal saga of Rajputana courage and supreme sacrifice.

2. **Sheesh Mahal (The Palace of Mirrors)**  
   A dazzling palace of over **2.5 million hand-cut concave mirrors**, brought to life using centuries-old Thikri craft, pure gold, gemstone embellishments, and chandeliers. Features the **world's only Sheesh Mahal with designed glass flooring**.

3. **The Royal Darbar**  
   An authentic 18th-century Rajputana courtroom adorned with pure gold and handcrafted miniature paintings, featuring lifelike figures of the Royal Family of Jaipur in full regalia.

4. **Hall of Icons**  
   A grand tribute to the heroes of India and the world across **42+ lifelike statues**, celebrating Bharat Ratnas, freedom fighters, scientists, sports legends, cinema pioneers, and a dedicated kids superhero realm.
